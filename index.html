Curriculum Roadmap

The 4-Phase Mastery Syllabus

A step-by-step progressive curriculum built from 8+ years of live market experience, engineered for real execution consistency.

PHASE 01 Foundation & Structure

Market Mechanics & Liquidity

Understanding how central banks, market makers, and liquidity pools actually move asset prices.

  • Market Structure: Break of Structure (BOS) vs Change of Character (CHoCH)
  • Buy-Side and Sell-Side Liquidity sweeps & stop hunt zones
  • Multi-timeframe top-down chart analysis (Daily to 5m)
  • Identifying premium vs discount equilibrium zones
PHASE 02 High-Winrate Setups

Order Flow & Price Action

Pinpoint precision entry models with minimum 1:3 to 1:5 risk-to-reward ratios.

  • High-probability Order Blocks & Fair Value Gaps (FVG)
  • Breaker blocks & mitigation setup confirmation
  • Volume profile & Point of Control (POC) integration
  • Session timing: London Open, New York Killzones
PHASE 03 Capital Preservation

Risk Management & Sizing

The math that keeps you profitable even with a 45% winrate. Eliminating blown accounts forever.

  • Strict 1% risk rule and position sizing spreadsheet
  • Leverage mastery: Cross vs Isolated, managing drawdown
  • Eliminating FOMO, revenge trading, and emotional tilting
  • Asymmetric compounding strategies for modest capital
PHASE 04 Live Floor Execution

Execution, Journaling & Scaling

Executing live setups with mentor oversight, auditing trade logs, and scaling trading capital.

  • Real-time live trading floor sessions with Nomi
  • Custom Notion Trade Journal setup & post-trade audits
  • Prop firm evaluation prep (FTMO, Funding Pips)
  • Transitioning to full-time or consistent second income
